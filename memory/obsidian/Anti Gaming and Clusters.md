# WalletIntel — Anti Gaming and Clusters

Documented filters exclude insufficient data and receive-only airdrop records from ranked trader claims, while other patterns flag wash, insider, MEV, dust or clustering risk. Detection thresholds and exclusion policies are versioned producer decisions; this graph does not rebuild or modify them.

Token-set similarity, common funders and same-transaction bundles can suggest coordination, but each has false positives. Taxonomy explicitly warns that a bundler need not be the developer and that single-metric labels are insufficient verdicts. Preserve transaction/funder/token evidence and confidence rather than turning association into guilt.

Later dust and insider override notes qualify early generic filters. Small transaction counts do not justify destroying provenance about a suspected insider; measurable notional, context and validated exceptions matter. Old identity rows remain in their source registry, not in the active vault.
