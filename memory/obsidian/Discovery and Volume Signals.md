# WalletIntel — Discovery and Volume Signals

The volume-sweep directive harvests wallets associated with significant token activity. It is a discovery/monitoring signal, not an entry recommendation. Even tokens marked rug-risk can supply useful developer, bundler, early-buyer or victim evidence for later enrichment.

The source proposes first/doubling/trough/sustained activity categories and explicitly distinguishes a revived old token from a fresh launch. Exact budgets, thresholds and claimed running state are dated records; no provider polling or wallet harvesting is performed by this curation.

Early volume against thin liquidity can indicate adverse conditions without proving any particular wallet's intent. Discovered wallet records must pass enrichment, identity, accounting and verification before producing eligibility evidence. Treat provider outage as missing data rather than a dead token, and keep discovery separate from a profitable-wallet assertion.
