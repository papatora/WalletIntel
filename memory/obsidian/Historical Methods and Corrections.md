# Historical methods and corrections

Historical wallet material preserves the questions behind eligibility: is PnL rederived, is apparent profit skill rather than market beta/insider allocation, and is the style consistent enough for copy latency? R1 independent quote/oracle checks, R2 raw-flow trade re-derivation and R3 stale-open treatment are separate evidentiary layers, not a single verified badge. The old tier examples remain dated examples, not current recommendations.

The technical notes distinguish transaction-level net flow from per-leg classification through routers, price orientation from quote denomination, fee-aware win from a gross return just above1x, and receive-only allocations from bought positions. The guide also qualifies security-grid and developer-panel labels: coordination alone is not developer ownership, locked liquidity is not inferred from its dollar size, and labels need evidence/confidence.

Funding clusters, developer registries, pump participants and wallet-scenario snapshots are now readable as local historical evidence. Their public identities and old annotations are preserved without asserting they remain accurate. Duplicate exports are represented once. The Python/dashboard snapshots are plain text, not installed programs, so their old imports or provider calls are not runtime dependencies.

The source chronology contains conflicting process/remote/publishing and sample-count snapshots. The September memory and October migration records must be read by date; restored infrastructure does not prove profitable eligibility. Credentials have been intentionally removed, and any private secret backup needed before deleting old folders is the user’s responsibility.
