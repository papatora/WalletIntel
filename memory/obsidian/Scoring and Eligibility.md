# WalletIntel — Scoring and Eligibility

The documented composite combines win rate, median/max returns, timing percentiles, breadth/consistency and recency. Those summaries rank hypotheses about wallet behavior; they are not guarantees that a delayed copy can reproduce historical results. Configuration owns weights and thresholds rather than vault prose.

The sources conflict by date: the earlier scoring document defines a winning position as multiple greater than 1.0, while later technical/eligibility memory describes fee-aware wins at or above 1.02. The migration does not inspect or change current scoring configuration, so it preserves the conflict rather than selecting a new active constant.

Eligibility additionally asks whether accounting is real, apparent profit is skill rather than market beta/insider allocation, and behavior is sufficiently consistent. Historical tier examples remain anecdotal and are excluded from this graph. Confirmed producer evidence cannot bypass TokenSniper's independent safety gate.
