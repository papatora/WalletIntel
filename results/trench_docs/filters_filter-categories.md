# Filter Categories

### Introduction

Once you create your filter, you'll be presented with all the categories you can toggle between. Feel free to experiment with what makes sense. Toggle, backtest, and assess. Run it as many times as necessary to reach your desired risk/reward appetite. \
\
We've designed our UI in such a way that if you're subscribed and have access, you'll *never* have to look at this page again. The UI has EVERYTHING highlighted, from each category to each individual option, with pointers, tips, and explanations on each, making the learning experience easy and seamless.\
\
This page is a quick preview of the categories, and may be outdated as we add more options in the future! &#x20;

### 👥 Insider Detection

Identify potential insider trading patterns

• Track connected wallet behaviors\
• Detect early buying before public launch\
• Monitor team wallet movements\
• Identify coordinated buying patterns\
\
Insiders wallets are essentially wallets that strictly buy pump.fun tokens before they bond. They're usually freshly funded or have a connection to other traders or the deployer team. Using them alone will net a lot of calls and likely bad winrates, but if paired with high volume or a whale, you can get very good results.

### :zap:Volume Thresholds

One thing that we excel at is tracking everything on chain. That alone has allowed us to monitor and filter out volumes in-house. We've added that into its own category where you're able to track volumes on *all timeframes*, and monitor for any suspicious spikes on any timeframe as well!

Keep in mind, we only monitor REAL volume from REAL wallets. We automatically discard any fake volume/tx propping bots using our algorithms. This means that the numbers you see may be a bit different from a dex/trading terminal, but it's much more valuable to track real trading when it comes to filtering.

#### :chart\_with\_upwards\_trend: :warning: Note about spikes!

In order to have a working spike detection system, you need to define both your volume spike % AND a spike timeframe. We track volume spikes on all timeframes and it didn't make sense to allow filtering for all of them from a technical standpoint. We recommend filtering for something higher than 5 minutes, as that's usually very volatile and can be triggered by just 1 whale selling or buying tokens, or set higher volume thresholds if you define 5 minutes.&#x20;

### ⚙️ Alert Preferences

Here you can define the frequency of alerts that you'd like to receive. If you have a volatile filter set in place, it's wise to have a lower alert frequency so that you don't get spammed. The alert frequency only checks SAME TOKENS.&#x20;

Meaning that if a token passes your filter, then quickly passes it again within the next 5 minutes, you won't get alerts for that token until the time has passed. You'll however still get alerts for any different tokens that pass regardless! Each token is tracked separately from its posting time and respects the frequency that you set!
